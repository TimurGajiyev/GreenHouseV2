6. Storage-type DVR System Design
6.1 System Positioning
The storage-type DVR is key to ensuring power quality. Sensitive loads (PLCs,
servo motors, automated lines) require voltage fluctuation≤±5%. The DVR is
connected in series between the bus and sensitive loads, detecting and
compensatingvoltagesags,swells,harmonics,etc.,inrealtime.

6.3 Working Principle
The DVR injects compensating voltage via a series transformer to keep load
voltage constant. In normal conditions, DVR is in standby (zero power draw). When
bus voltage exceeds the threshold, within 5 ms the DVR discharges storage and
injectscompensationvoltage,keepingloadvoltagefluctuationwithin±5%.

8. System Operation Strategies
8.1 Five Weather Condition Strategies and Fault Modes
Condition 1 – Sunny, high irradiance (PV at full output)
PVoutput≥700kW,fullymeetingload.ExcessPVchargesstorage,SOCrises.If
storageisfullandPVstillhassurplus,MPPTcurtailmentreducesPVoutput.
Condition 2 – Cloudy, intermittent (PV fluctuating)
PV output 300–600 kW. Storage fills the gap; SVG compensates short-term
fluctuations.EMSdynamicallyadjustsstoragepowertokeepbusstable.
Condition 3 – Overcast/rainy (low PV output)
PV output ≤200 kW, mainly storage power. When SOC drops to threshold (e.g.,
30%), EMS auto-starts DG to parallel, DG takes main load and charges storage. DG
exitswhenSOCreaches80%.
Condition 4 – Sandstorm/extreme weather (PV nearly zero)
PV negligible, storage alone. When SOC reaches 20%, DG starts. If SOC falls
below5%,storageDVRintervenes(upto20min)untilDGstabilizes,thentransfersto
DG.
Condition 5 – PV or storage system fault
Regardless, if bothPV and storagefail, DG responds immediately (≤5 ms), DVR
supports(upto20min)untilDGstabilizes,thentransferstoDGemergencysupply.
Mode A – Normal state (applicable to Conditions 1, 2)

maintenance, manual closing of switches, exit isolation mode, EMS decides whether
to exit DG. If DG exits, DVR connects, storage island mode supplies power, voltage
stabilizes,PVconnects,DVRdisconnects,thenstorage+PVsupply.

13. Conclusion
This microgrid solution for the Sana'a factory adopts a four-in-one hybrid energy
architecture: "PV + storage + diesel + storage-type DVR", achieving the following
objectives:
• Uninterruptedpowersupply:Coversallfiveweatherconditions,guaranteeing
powerfor10workinghours.
• Powerquality:DVRensurestransfertime≤5msandsupport≥20minduring
voltagedips/transients;SVGprovidesvoltagecompensationtokeepsensitive
loadvoltagefluctuationwithin±5%.
• Economicoptimality:PV≥1500kWp+BESS≥3132kWhwithbest
cost-performanceratio.
• Intelligentdispatch:Grid-forming PCS+EMSforfullyautomaticenergy
management.
• Safetyandreliability:Multi-levelprotection,black-startcapability,remote
monitoring.
• Greenandlow-carbon:~8,000tonsCO₂reductionperyear,reduceddiesel
dependence.
This proposal fully considersYemen's local climate (low latitude, highirradiation,
occasional sandstorms) and infrastructure conditions, using mature and reliable
technology with modular design to ensure stable operation and economic returns
throughoutthemicrogrid'slifecycle.
Through deep AC coupling of PV and grid-forming storage, supplemented by
diesel and storage-type DVR backup, this system achieves 10 hours of continuous
high-quality power supply and seamless production under all weather conditions,
meetingtheplant'shigh-qualityprocessrequirements.
